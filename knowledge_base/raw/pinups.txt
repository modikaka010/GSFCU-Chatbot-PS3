President’s Initiative for Nurturing Unlimited Potentials of Students (PINUPS)
PINUPS
A key mission of GSFC University is to enhance the quality of the graduating student experience. Students aim to expand their knowledge and skills throughout their academic journey, preparing for successful and fulfilling careers. In this endeavor, teachers play a crucial role by offering compassionate support, fostering an environment where students feel comfortable and encouraged to share their concerns openly, thus facilitating effective communication.
GSFC University is dedicated to discover ‘Unlimited Potential’ of the students creating industry-ready, employable graduates through a holistic, student-centric approach. The concept of 'Unlimited Potential' in education captures the profound, often hidden dimensions of student development, reaching well beyond traditional categorizations. It recognizes the vast array of talents, interests, and personal traits unique to each student, ready to be discovered and nurtured. This idea underscores the expansive possibilities within every individual, going beyond the boundaries of established labels or activities.
Recognizing this vision, the President of GSFC University is deeply invested in the future of the students and the institution’s role in achieving this goal. As a result, there is a pressing need for a unique initiative aimed at identifying and nurturing the top 1% of students. These high-achievers would receive tailored guidance, support, and mentoring through special programs, ensuring they stand out and make a significant impact in their future careers.
Implementation:
PINUPS Selection Framework Overview
Phase I- Orientation:
At the onset of the Academic Year 2024-2025, the PINUPS Team will initiate Orientation sessions, guiding students through the PINUPS initiative and its objectives, particularly tailored for first-year students.
Phase II- Expression of Interest:
Students will express their interest in PINUPS through a formal process
Phase III- The committee will review the expression of interest forms submitted by students and identify those who have shown interest in showcasing their talents. The committee will conduct interviews for shortlisted students/year/program . Subsequently, students/year/program will be recommended by the committee to progress to the next round, which is the talent showcase.
Phase IV- Talent Show:
The talent show provides students with an opportunity to showcase their wide-ranging talents and creativity, contributing to the creation of a positive and inclusive campus atmosphere that encourages participation and appreciation for diverse forms of talent.
Phase V- Personal Interview (PI):
To ensure fairness and thoroughness, PINUPS 2.0 will also undergo a Personal Interview (PI) as part of the selection process.
Phase VI- Formation of President's Meritorious Group (PMG):
The students selected through the PINUPS process are known as the President's Meritorious Group (PMG). ~1% of the total intake will be selected based on above mentioned criteria, ensuring a diverse and well-rounded group.
Phase VII- Allocating students to the Mentors:
Each participating student will be paired with an internal mentor from their respective school. The role of these internal mentors will then connect them with an external mentor who specializes in their particular area of expertise.
Phase VIII- Self- Assessment by the students:
Students chosen for mentoring will be requested to complete a comprehensive Self-Assessment form. This form is designed to delve into various aspects, including their personality traits, goals, aspirations, and expectations from the university. This approach ensures that the mentoring process is tailored to each student's unique needs and aligns with their personal and academic growth objectives.
Phase IX- Continuous Mentoring:
Assigned mentors play a crucial role in offering continuous guidance and support to students throughout the academic year, nurturing both personal and professional development.
Phase X- Feedback:
Continuous improvement is integral to the PINUPS initiative, with a commitment to refining and enhancing its success based on valuable feedback garnered from the previous year. This iterative refinement process ensures that the program evolves and adapts to better meet the needs of the students and align with its overarching goals.
