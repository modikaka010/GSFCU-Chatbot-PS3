Minor Degree in Core Engineering Program
Overview
With a view to enhance the employability skills and impart knowledge in emerging areas, “Minor Degree” is offer to Core Engineering program (B.Tech Chemical B.Tech F & EHS and B.Tech Mechanical).
Objective
In order to achieve the above GSFC university has made Industrial Training (internship) a part of continuous education by
Encourage advanced levels of study and multidisciplinary learning
Specialize in a focus area of interest
Learn new skills and methodologies about an area of interest
Enable Employability
Cultivate a sustaining interest in advanced education and research
Enhance professional development leading to guaranteed employment
Provide opportunity to gain knowledge and skill in the emerging areas which will be reflected in students’ credentials.
Framework
Any student desirous to opt for Minor Degree in any of the specializations, needs to earn additional 18 credits (over and above the required 190-195 credits for BTech degree).
