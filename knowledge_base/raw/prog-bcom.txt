About The Program
The Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) Program at GSFC University (GSFCU) provides students with a strong foundation in core business principles while preparing them for success in a rapidly evolving global economy. The program combines conceptual knowledge with practical applications, enabling students to develop competencies in accounting, finance, economics, and business law. Graduates are equipped to pursue careers across corporate, public, and entrepreneurial sectors with confidence and professionalism.

Through a comprehensive curriculum, students acquire the knowledge and tools required to effectively analyze business problems, evaluate financial strategies, and contribute to organizational success.
The faculty members, with extensive academic and industry experience, provide personalized mentoring to help students realize their full potential. The curriculum emphasizes

With a systematic approach to career development, the B.Com. program at GSFCU ensures that students are not only equipped with academic knowledge but also with the practical exposure and skills needed to thrive in their careers. The program offers a transformative learning experience that enhances business acumen, nurtures entrepreneurial thinking, and fosters leadership qualities essential for success in today’s global business environment.

At the end of the B.Com. programme, students will possess the ability to articulate, illustrate, analyze, synthesize, and apply the knowledge of principles and frameworks of commerce, accounting, and allied domains to the solutions of real-world business and financial challenges.
Graduates will be able to identify, analyze, and design innovative solutions to business and financial challenges using quantitative and qualitative analytical tools in commerce and accounting.
The students will possess the ability to investigate multidimensional business and financial problems using research-based knowledge and methods to arrive at data- driven and evidence-based decisions in commerce and accounting.
Graduates will be able to effectively communicate in diverse cultural settings and technology-driven environments, particularly in commerce-related contexts and with society at large.
The curriculum develops the ability to collaborate in an organizational context and across organizational boundaries and lead themselves and others in the achievement of organizational goals and optimize outcomes for all stakeholders.
The program ensures that students approach business and financial issues from a global perspective while demonstrating an understanding and appreciation of cross- cultural aspects of commerce and trade.
The students will be able to identify entrepreneurial opportunities and apply leadership and managerial skills to establish, lead, and manage startups, as well as professionalize and expand family businesses in the field of commerce.

Program Structure
The B.Com / B.Com (Honours) program spans three years (six semesters) for the degree and four years (eight semesters) for the Honours track, in accordance with the NEP 2020 framework.
The teaching-learning process includes interactive lectures, case studies, simulations, field visits, and industry internships. Each semester comprises a minimum of 15 weeks of academic engagement.Evaluation consists of Continuous Internal Assessment (60%) and End-Semester Examination (40%). Internal assessment includes quizzes, assignments, presentations, and projects under the Continuous Evaluation Component (CEC).
Evaluation
The assessment broadly comprises of Internal Assessment (Continuous Evaluation Component) and External Assessment i.e., End Semester Examination. Each course carries 100 marks for theory with 60 marks for Internal Assessment and 40 for End Semester Examination and the practical course carries an additional 50 marks. The internal assessment (CEC - Continous Evaluation Components) will be through MCQ, Classroom Tests, Assignments, Oral Presentations, and Short Projects.
Courses
The curriculum has been designed in accordance with the AICTE-prescribed curriculum framework. The program consists of Major (Core) Courses (CC) of four credits each, Minor (Elective) Courses of four credits each, Multi/Inter-Disciplinary Courses (MDC) of four credits each, Ability Enhancement Courses (AEC) of two credits each, Skill Enhancement Courses (SEC) of two credits each and Value-Added Course/Indian Knowledge System (VAC/IKS) of two credits each.
For theory classes, one credit indicates one hour lecture per week while for practical, one credit indicates two hours of practical laboratory session per week.

Eligibility for Admission
Candidates who have completed 10+2 (Commerce stream) or equivalent from a recognized board with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) are eligible for admission. Admission is based on merit and a first-come, first-served basis. Applicants from international boards (IB, IGCSE, etc.) must submit an equivalence certificate issued by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), New Delhi.
Registration Fees
The application fee is ₹500, payable online.
Selection Process:
50% and above aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - Direct admission on first cum first basis, 45% for SC/ST/OBC-Non-Creamy Layer/PD/EWS.
