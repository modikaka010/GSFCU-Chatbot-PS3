Vichar Vimarsh
In alignment with GSFC University’s vision of fostering academic excellence, interdisciplinary growth and future-ready education, a dedicated knowledge-exchange platform titled
“Vichar -Vimarsh”
has been introduced for all teaching and non-teaching staff. This initiative has been designed to promote reflective academic practices, pedagogical innovations, collaborative research culture and integrated professional development across all schools and departments.
The forum serves as a collaborative space for intellectual dialogue, experience sharing, and deliberation on transformative themes that align with
NEP 2020, Education 4.0, Skill India
and the
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
Objectives:
To cultivate a reflective, research-oriented and collaborative academic environment.
To promote inclusive, evidence-based and interdisciplinary teaching-learning practices.
To build awareness and capacity around digital pedagogies, academic research, institutional innovation and well-being.
To involve faculty and administrative staff in holistic institutional development through structured discussions and thematic sessions.
To serve as a platform for strategic insights on curriculum design, student mentoring, research funding, and academic leadership
Coordinator:
Ms. Priyanka Pandya, Sr. Assistant Professor, Chemical Engineering Dept.
A Journey from Academia to Industry, 4th Oct 2025
An engaging Vichar Vimarsh session was conducted by Dr. Prabal Sengupta on “A Journey from Academia to Industry: From Discovery to Development” The talk provided valuable insights into the definition of drugs, the key stages of drug development, and recent advances shaping modern pharmaceutical research.
Dr. Sengupta also discussed the pre-clinical and clinical development process and shared an inspiring case study.
The session offered a thoughtful blend of scientific knowledge and real-world application, deepening our understanding of how innovative research leads to life-changing therapies.
Crafting a scholar-Teacher Identity: A path to a Balanced Success, 2nd August 2025
An inspiring session on "Crafting a Scholar-Teacher Identity: A Path to Balanced Success," Professor G R Sinha, Provost (Vice Chancellor) shared his remarkable Academic journey and valuable insights for faculty development.
Emphasizing the importance of continuous learning, he encouraged educators to seamlessly integrate teaching with research, transform classrooms into dynamic knowledge hubs and remain guided by both internal purpose and societal relevance.
Key reflections included the value of teaching beyond Geographical and disciplinary boundaries, and the importance of building a visible scholarly presence. It was an enriching Vichar Vimarsh session that ignited intellectual curiosity and purpose-driven teaching in all of us.
