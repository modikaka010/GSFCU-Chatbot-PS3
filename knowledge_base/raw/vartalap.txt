VARTALAP
In line with the NEP 2020 guidelines and with the aim of nurturing a spirit of scientific curiosity and innovation among students, GSFC University has introduced
“VARTALAP”
- an expert lecture series for students. This academic forum is an
integral component of ongoing academic activities. The aim of VARTALAP is to bring forth cutting-edge research, recent innovations and real-world scientific applications
through a series of thought-provoking lectures and discussions.
The forum invites eminent personalities from academia, industry professionals, successful young entrepreneurs with scientific start-up journeys and experienced resource persons. The lecture series is intended to be conducted once or twice a month after the regular academic sessions of students and will be curated to ensure relevance and value to students across all programs.
To ensure holistic involvement and smooth execution, faculty members and students across all three Schools School of Technology (SoT), School of Science (SoS) and School of Management & Liberal Arts (SoM&LA) will play an active role in organizing, coordinating and facilitating these sessions.
Objectives:
To foster a culture of scientific curiosity and innovation
To facilitate interdisciplinary knowledge exchange
To enrich academic engagement beyond classrooms
To bridge the gap between theoretical learning and practical application
To provide mentorship and inspiration
To ensure holistic participation
To strengthen the University’s academic ecosystem
Coordinator:
Ms. Priyanka Pandya, Sr. Assistant Professor, Chemical Engineering Dept.
A Somatic Embryogenesis and Its Applications on 1st Oct 2025
GSFC University successfully hosted an inspiring Expert Talk on “Somatic Embryogenesis and Its Applications” by Dr. Ghanshyam Bhaurao Patil, Head & I/c Associate Research Scientist, Centre for Advanced Research in Plant Tissue Culture, Anand Agricultural University. Dr. Patil highlighted the importance of somatic embryogenesis in plant propagation, synthetic seed production, and germplasm conservation, while discussing somaclonal variation as both a challenge and an opportunity for crop improvement. The session effectively linked theory with practice, inspiring innovative thinking in sustainable biotechnology and enhancing participants’ understanding through an engaging Q&A.

Project Management with Financial Understanding on 29th Jul 2025
Under the VARTALAP forum, an expert session on “Project Management with Financial Understanding” was conducted by Shri Bimal Bhayani Professor of Practice at GSFCU. The session offered practical insights into project execution, with a focus on financial planning, project costing, and feasibility. A detailed case study on revamping and refurbishing projects helped students understand real-world challenges. Key financial concepts like Break-even Analysis, Compounded Savings Value (CSV), and the impact of inflation were also introduced, making the session both insightful and career-relevant. Students appreciated the industry-aligned approach and valuable learning experience.
