Internship
GSFC University- Nestled in the lush green surroundings of Fertilizernagar, GSFC University is a boutique institution designed to produce industry-ready talent, with 80% of students securing employment. Inspired by medical education, where theory meets practice in hospitals, our campus is located steps away from GSFC Limited housing 22 plants, allowing students to apply classroom knowledge directly to real-world industry.
We follow a four-pillar learning approach
Virtual Labs – Master instruments and experiments in a simulated environment to understand the “why” behind theory.
Physical Labs – Hands-on experiments with real fluids and lab equipment bring theory to life.
Plant Exposure – Observe and interact with industrial machinery, linking classroom learning to industry operations.
Internship Immersion – Six months of in-depth plant training, giving students real-world industry experience.
To achieve Industry-Ready Competence, GSFC University Integrates Industrial Training (Internship as a core component of continuous education through:
4 weeks of hands-on training after each semester
A structured syllabus with well-defined learning outcomes
A 2-credit assessment that contributes meaningfully to the final evaluation
At GSFC University, theory meets practice, preparing students to excel in the industry from day one.
