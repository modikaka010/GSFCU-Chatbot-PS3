1. General Provisions
1.1 The University Management reserves the right to cancel any admission granted
inadvertently by the Admission Department or obtained by a candidate on the
basis of incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information.
7.2 All admitted candidates are required to strictly adhere to the rules, regulations,
discipline, and code of conduct of the University. This includes conduct during
academic activities, study tours, industrial visits, internships, workshops, and any
other engagements within or outside the campus.
Failure to comply may result in cancellation of admission at any stage, at the sole
discretion of the University.

5. Refund of Fees fExisting Students)
5.1 No refund of fees shall be admissible once the admission is confirmed and the

5.3 Refund of any deposit shall be at the discretion of the University Management.
