About The Program
The B.Tech program in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) is designed to build a robust foundation in electronics while equipping students with practical skills in cutting-edge domains. The curriculum fosters
innovation, analytical thinking, and research orientation, preparing graduates to address real-world engineering challenges
With Gujarat rapidly emerging as a key hub in India’s
electronics and semiconductor ecosystem, and the development of a semiconductor manufacturing and design belt
in and around the state, the program is strategically aligned with regional and national priorities. It also supports flagship initiatives such as India Semiconductor Mission led by Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, which aim to strengthen the country’s semiconductor design, manufacturing, and innovation capabilities. This alignment ensures that students are well-prepared for opportunities in the rapidly growing semiconductor and electronics sectors.
The program emphasizes holistic student development through active participation in technical clubs, student chapters, and co-curricular activities. Continuous mentoring is provided through structured training in soft skills, placement readiness, and preparation for competitive examinations via foundation and bridge courses.
Electronics and Communication Engineering is a dynamic and evolving discipline driven by
advancements in VLSI design, communication systems, IoT, and intelligent technologies
. A strong grounding in fundamentals, combined with exposure to emerging trends, is essential for future engineers.
To address these
industry demands
, the program offers the following specialization tracks:
1. VLSI Design & Technology
2. Information & Communication Technology (ICT)
Program Outcomes
Engineering knowledge:
Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.
Problem analysis:
Identify, formulate, research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.
Design/development of solutions:
Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet the specified needs with appropriate consideration for the public health and safety, and the cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.
Conduct investigations of complex problems:
Use research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of the information to provide valid conclusion
Modern tool usage:
Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools including prediction and modelling to complex engineering activities with an understanding of the limitations.
The engineer and society:
Apply reasoning informed by the contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to the professional engineering practice.
Environment and sustainability:
Understand the impact of the professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, and demonstrate the knowledge of, and need for sustainable development.
Ethics:
Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of the engineering practice.
Individual and team work:
Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams, and in multidisciplinary settings.
Communication:
Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and with the society at large, such as, being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations, and give and receive clear instructions.
Project management and finance:
Demonstrate knowledge understanding of the engineering and management principles and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.
Life-long learning:
Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

Program Structure
The B.Tech. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) program is of four years’ duration. Each year constitutes an academic year and is divided into two semesters, making a total of eight semesters. Each semester comprises approximately fifteen weeks of teaching and academic activities.
The teaching–learning process includes theory sessions of one-hour duration and practical/laboratory sessions of two-hour duration. The curriculum is delivered through a blend of pedagogical approaches such as classroom teaching, laboratory experiments, simulation-based learning, and digital tools.
Various methods are adopted to enhance learning, including lectures, hands-on laboratory work, use of EDA and simulation tools, audio-visual aids, e-learning platforms, virtual laboratories, industry visits, expert lectures, seminars, workshops, project-based learning, and industrial internships. This comprehensive approach ensures that students develop both strong theoretical foundations and practical competencies in electronics and communication engineering.
Evaluation
The assessment broadly comprises Internal Assessment (Continuous Evaluation Component) and External Assessment i.e., End Semester Examination. Each course carries 100 marks for theory with 60 marks for Internal Assessment and 40 for End Semester Examination and the practical course carries additional 50 marks. The internal assessment will be through MCQ, Classroom Test, Open Book Assignment, Oral Presentation and Short Projects.
Courses
The curriculum structure of the B.Tech. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) program is designed based on the fundamental requirements of electronics and communication engineering. The program comprises
Fundamental Engineering Courses, Core Courses, Professional Electives, NPTEL Electives, Open Electives, Ability and Skill Enhancement Courses, and Value-Added Courses
, ensuring a comprehensive and industry-relevant academic framework.
For theory classes, one credit corresponds to one hour of lecture per week, while for practical sessions, one credit corresponds to two hours of laboratory work per week.
The Fundamental Engineering Courses and Core Courses (CC) are mandatory for all students. The program offers three categories of elective courses: Professional Electives, NPTEL Electives, and Open Electives, enabling students to tailor their learning in alignment with emerging technologies and career aspirations. In addition, students undertake Ability Enhancement Compulsory Courses (AECC), Skill Enhancement Compulsory Courses (SECC), and Value-Added Courses to enhance technical and professional competencies.
The Core Courses, Discipline-Specific Electives, and Generic Electives typically carry 3 to 5 credits each, while Skill Enhancement and Ability Enhancement Courses are generally of 2 credits each. A student is required to earn a minimum of 175 credits to be eligible for the award of the B.Tech degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering.
The program offers specialization tracks from the 3rd semester onwards, allowing students to build focused expertise in the following domains.
● VLSI Design & Technology
● Information & Communication Technology (ICT)
Students can further strengthen their specialization through Professional Electives offered in the 6th and 7th semesters, along with an Open Elective in the 6th semester.

Admission Process
Total Intake: 30 Seats
Mode of Admission: Merit-based
Qualifying Examination:10+2 (Science) or equivalent
Eligibility for Admission
For State Quota Seats:
The State Quota seats of the B.Tech program shall be filled through the Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC), Government of Gujarat, as per prevailing guidelines.
For All India Seats (Management Quota):
The All India quota seats of the B.Tech program shall be filled by GSFC University based on the following eligibility criteria:
- Seats (including applications from outside Gujarat) will be filled from All India candidates (including Gujarat) on the basis of the NTA score in JEE (Main) – 2026 or the percentile rank of GUJCET 2026 examinations. Admission notifications for these seats will be published on the University website.
- In case seats remain vacant under JEE (Main) or GUJCET categories, such seats may be interchanged between the two categories as per university norms.
- The candidate must have passed the HSC (Science stream) or an equivalent qualifying examination with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks (55% for SC/ST/SEBC) in theory and practical of Physics and Mathematics, along with Chemistry / Computer / Vocational subject from a recognized board.
