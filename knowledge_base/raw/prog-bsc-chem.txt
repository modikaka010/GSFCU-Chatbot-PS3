About The Program
Embark on a transformative journey where the boundless potential of science merges with the realms of technology and engineering. As the world witnesses rapid advancements on the national and international stage, the demand for profound understanding and application of basic sciences has never been greater. At GSFC University, we stand unwaveringly committed to upholding exceptional academic standards.
Welcome to the esteemed School of Science at GSFC University, where we proudly present an awe-inspiring B.Sc. Chemistry Program, meticulously crafted to align with the visionary
National Education Policy (NEP) 2020
. This program has been ingeniously designed, drawing inspiration from the revered
"Curriculum and Credit Framework for Undergraduate Programs"
recommended by the UGC. It seamlessly integrates a flexible choice-based credit system and an invigorating multidisciplinary approach, igniting a true passion for learning.
The B.Sc. Chemistry Program spans over eight semesters (four years), strategically laying a strong foundational understanding of subjects, while delving into practical applications and recent developments. Every semester, students have the unique opportunity to immerse themselves in industrial internships, acquiring both theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience.
Our specialized chemistry courses cater to the demands of a dynamic industrial landscape, setting the stage for prosperous careers in research and development, industries, and academic institutions. Furthermore, the institute may provide placement opportunities to our aspiring graduates.
Our B.Sc. Chemistry Program offers an array of degree options, allowing students to customize their academic journey according to their aspirations:
• 3 Year B.Sc. Chemistry
• 4 Year B.Sc. Chemistry – Honours
• 4 Year B.Sc. Chemistry – Honours with Research

Program Objectives
To equip students with a strong foundation in chemistry, enabling them to analyze, formulate, and effectively solve real-life problems.
To foster professionalism, ethical values, communication skills, teamwork, and a multidisciplinary approach among students, promoting their continuous educational growth and professional development.
To prepare students for successful careers in various domains such as industry, technology, research, and higher education/academia, instilling in them a sense of creativity and unwavering commitment.
To empower students with the ability to apply their knowledge towards understanding the intricacies of nature, humanity, and sustainable development, thereby enhancing their skills in translation and synthesis.
Program Outcomes
Students will acquire and apply a comprehensive understanding of scientific concepts, including chemistry, physics, mathematics, organic chemistry, inorganic chemistry, physical chemistry, analytical chemistry, and computer applications in chemistry, to effectively address challenges within the field.
Students will demonstrate proficiency in designing, executing, and analyzing experiments, enabling them to investigate intricate problems in chemistry and related disciplines.
Students will possess the skills necessary to develop innovative and sustainable solutions to significant environmental issues, utilizing appropriate tools and methodologies within the realm of chemistry.
Students will exhibit strong collaborative abilities, showcasing their aptitude to work as part of a team within multidisciplinary environments.
Students will demonstrate effective written and verbal communication skills, effectively conveying their ideas and findings in a clear and concise manner.
Students will have a deep comprehension of ethical standards and norms within the field of chemistry, demonstrating their knowledge and adherence to professional ethics.
Program Structure
The
B.Sc. Chemistry
Program has a duration of
four years
, with the option for students to
exit after three years
. Each academic year is divided into two semesters, resulting in a total of eight semesters. The duration of each semester is fifteen weeks, dedicated to teaching and learning.
The teaching-learning process incorporates both theory classes, which are one hour in duration, and practical classes, which are four hours long. The curriculum delivery encompasses various methods, including traditional chalk and talk lectures, hands-on laboratory sessions, dynamic presentations using audio and video tools, engaging e-learning and e-content resources, virtual laboratories, simulations, field trips, industry visits, seminars, workshops, projects, interactive models, class discussions, and invaluable industrial internships.
Evaluation methods encompass both internal and external assessments. The assessment structure comprises Internal Assessment, which is a continuous evaluation component, and External Assessment, specifically the End Semester Examination. Each course is allocated a total of 100 marks for theory, with 50 marks allocated for Internal Assessment and 50 marks for the End Semester

Eligibility for Admission
Candidate must have passed 12th or equivalent (Science Stream) Examination from the recognized Board excluding NIOS securing a minimum 40% mark in aggregate of all the subjects. Applicants with 12th or equivalent from International Schools, affiliated to foreign education boards like IGCSE (International General Certificate for Secondary Education) and IB (International Baccalaureate) can apply, they have to submit equivalence certificate of Association of Indian Universities, New Delhi.
Registration Fees
The registration fee for submitting online application form is Rs. 500/- (Rupees Five Hundred Only), which is to be paid online.
Selection Process:
45% and above aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - Direct admission on first cum first basis. 40% to 45% aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - on the spot Admission after interaction with the Faculty, School of Science, GSFC University.
