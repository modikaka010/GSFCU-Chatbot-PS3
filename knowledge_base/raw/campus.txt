Campus life
GSFC University
The University is located on the outskirts of multicultural, cosmopolitan Vadodara city. Campus lies within the sprawling and lush green surroundings of GSFC which enables students to relax, interact and study. Surroundings of the campus and girls' hostel within it make it girls friendly. Proximity of the Campus from the battery limit of GSFC enables practical learning and facilitates industrial internship.
