About GSFCU
Library
The GSFC University Library’s mission is to connect people to the world of information and innovative ideas and it is the state-of-art of Resource Centre enriched with information resources in the field of Engineering, Science and Technology, Chemical Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Fire Safety, Chemistry , Biotechnology, Management and all other related subjects. The main objective of GSFC University Library is to identify, acquire, organize, store, and provide on-demand access of intellectual information to its faculty, staff, students and other users.
