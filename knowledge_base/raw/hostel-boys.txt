VIKRAM SARABHAI BHAVAN / SWAMI VIVEKANAND BHAVAN
FOR BOYS
Boy's hostel inaugurated by Honorable Chief Minister, Shri Vijaybhai Rupani on 7th June 2018. GSFC University offers comfortable and convenient residence for boys students. The residential complex provides accommodation on sharing basis and ensures safety and well-being for all boys living there. The complex is designed to give the residents a sense of home and living in big family.
