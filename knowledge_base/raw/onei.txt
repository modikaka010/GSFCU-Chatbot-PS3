Online Exam Initiative (ONEI)
GSFC University has taken the initiative to conduct “open book” online mid-semester and end semester examinations. The open book examination is the assessment method that allows students to use certain resources such as textbooks, lecture notes, etc during the examination. Google classroom was used as the platform to upload question paper and submission of answer copy. The main premise for open book examination is that faculties can devise questions that require students to answer in a more critical and analytical way thus encouraging high- order thinking skills in the students
