About The Program
Bachelor of Business Administration - Business Analytics (BBA-BA) is the process of interpreting data to help organizations make informed, strategic decisions. It involves the collection, analysis, and interpretation of large datasets from both profit and non-profit organizations. As a rapidly emerging discipline, Business Analytics has become a vital component of management science, where decision-making is largely data-driven. In view of, huge data across functional areas like marketing, finance, Production, Purchase, HR, Logistics. The complexity of data is increasing with the growth of the business and competition in the concerned sector. Therefore, Business analytics is becoming an effective tool in dealing with decision making which is complex in terms of magnitude of data.

Program Structure
The BBA (Business Analytics) program follows the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 framework, offering a three-year undergraduate degree with an optional fourth year (Honours/Research). Each academic year is divided into two semesters, totaling six to eight semesters. Thus, there will be a total of six to eight semesters. Each semester includes a minimum of fifteen weeks of instruction, combining theory and practical sessions. Teaching–learning methods include interactive lectures, case studies, simulations, data labs, virtual learning environments, industry visits, projects, and internships.
Evaluation
Evaluation consists of Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and End-Semester Examination (ESE).
Each theory course carries 100 marks: 60 for internal components (assignments, quizzes, case studies, presentations) and 40 for end-semester examination.
Practical and project-based courses carry separate evaluation components based on performance, reports, and viva-voce..

Mode of Admission

Qualifying Examination

Eligibility for Admission
Candidates who have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination in any stream (Commerce/Science/Arts) from a recognized board with at least 45% aggregate marks (40% for reserved categories) are eligible to apply. Admissions are granted on a merit and first-come, first-served basis. Applicants from international boards such as IB or IGCSE must submit an equivalence certificate issued by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), New Delhi.
Registration Fees
The application fee is ₹500, payable online
Selection Process:
50% and above aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - Direct admission on first cum first basis, 45% for SC/ST/OBC-Non-Creamy Layer/PD/EWS.
