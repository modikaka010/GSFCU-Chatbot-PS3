Student's Managed Clubs
Student's Managed Clubs (SMC) promotes the culture of active participative learning and provides a firm platform to the students for sharing their innovative ideas. Various clubs includes activities that promote social responsibility, civic engagement, industrial understanding, projects management, meticulous documentation and project execution with a purpose to train and develop human capital. The concept of initiating Students Managed Clubs (SMC) is an integral part of GSFC University to encourage active participation of students and thereby promote healthy competition.
Existing clubs of GSFC University are
: Aarogyam Club, Adventure Club, Biotechnology Club, Chemistry Club, Code Club, Community Service Club, Dance Club, Debate and Elocution Club, Disaster Risk Reduction Club, Elite Management Club, Entrepreneurship Club, Environment Club, Indian Heritage Club, Mass Communication & Media Club, Mathematics Club, Music Club, Photography Club, Smart Data Club, Sports Club, Theater Club.
Aarogyam Club
The Aarogyam Club is a student-managed initiative at GSFC University dedicated to promoting physical, mental, and social well-being within the campus community. The club organizes health awareness programs, fitness activities, wellness talks, and outreach drives to encourage healthy lifestyle practices among students and staff. Its objective is to create a culture of preventive healthcare, mindfulness, and overall wellness. By engaging students in leadership roles and peer-to-peer learning, the club fosters responsibility, teamwork, and a sense of service. The impact of Aarogyam Club is reflected in increased health awareness, improved participation in wellness activities, and a supportive environment for holistic development.
Objectives:
Promote preventive healthcare and wellness practices within the campus.
Organize awareness programs on lifestyle diseases and public health issues.
Encourage mindfulness, mental health care, and stress management among students.
Foster leadership, teamwork, and service through student-led health initiatives.
Build a supportive and health-conscious campus environment that nurtures holistic development.

Disaster Risk Reduction Club
The Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) Club at GSFC University is a proactive platform promoting disaster awareness, preparedness, and resilience among students and faculty. In addition to students from the Fire & EHS Department, the club has engaged students from all other departments, including MBA, CSE, and Chemical Engg., who take on lead roles in initiatives and events. Aligned with the Prime Minister’s 10-Point Agenda on DRR (Agenda 6), the NIDM’s IUIN–DRR network, and the UGC directive (D.O. No. 15-19/2025), the club advances its vision through expert talks, hands-on training, and awareness activities that build a safe, sustainable, and resilient campus community.
Objectives:-
Promote a culture of disaster preparedness and resilience on campus.
Conduct hands-on training and awareness programs including drills, First Aid, and CPR.
Engage students in community outreach and sustainability initiatives related to disaster management.
Develop leadership, teamwork, and volunteerism through active participation in DRR activities.
Collaborate with authorities and ensure campus safety through risk assessments and infrastructure preparedness.
