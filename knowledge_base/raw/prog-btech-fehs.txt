About The Program
In India, Fire services are constitutionally mandated under the 12th schedule, Article 243W entrusting Municipalities with the responsibility of Fire prevention and Fire fighting.. The States, Union territories (UTs), Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) currently manage these critical services. According to Accidental Deaths and Suicides Statistics India report, of 2022,a total of 7566 cases of fire accidents were reported in the country, and 53% were reported in residential and dwellings. Many of these deaths could have been easily prevented, had we been aware of and taken enough Fire Prevention, Life Safety and Fire Protection measures.
The vision of the University is to develop one of its kind Compact & Boutique University with industry-relevant education. The ultimate objective is to ensure “Future Ready” and “Highly Employable” students graduating from the university. GSFC University is the
first University in Gujarat to introduce B.Tech in Fire & Environment, Health, Safety program (F&EHS)
from the academic year 2019-20. This is the most socially relevant program contributing to the safety and security of valuable resources of the nation.
Data from the Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health (DISH) indicates that the number of registered factories in Gujarat increased from 35,338 in 2019 to 47,762 in 2024, with 953 classified as Major Accident Hazard (MAH) installations, dominated by the chemical sector. The recent fire accidents within and outside Gujarat emphasize the need for skilled and trained Fire & Safety Professionals.
The course curriculum is meticulously designed and revised regularly after obtaining input from the experts in the field of Fire, Safety, Health, and Environment. The course curriculum of B.Tech Fire and Environment, Health, Safety, not only matches with top Fire & Safety institutes, but also incorporates contemporary market requirements.
Moreover, a state-of-the-art training facility, namely
FirePlex (AgniSankul), A Centre of Excellence (CoE)
for Fire & EHS has been established by GSFC University in September 2023 at a cost of 2.5 crores rupees for imparting practical training and live demonstrations to the students and working professionals in Major Accident Hazard Industries. The FirePlex is equipped with a fire drill ground, drill tower, fire pump house, sprinkler system, foam room/ Medium/High velocity sprinkler system, Fire alarm system, and smoke chamber. This is complemented by Separate modern fire and safety labs equipped with the latest equipment. GSFC University has developed training facilities at a single location that may not be found elsewhere in Gujarat.

Program Specific Outcomes
Graduates will be able to apply principles of fire dynamics, fire science, Structural Fire behavior, Safety Engineering, Hazard and risk assessment methodologies to identify hazards, analyze different fire scenarios, and design fire prevention and protection systems in compliance with national and international codes, standards, and legislations.
Graduates will be able to design, audit, evaluate, and implement integrated occupational health, process safety, and industrial safety management systems, ensuring regulatory compliance, risk reduction, loss of human life and asset integrity across Hazardous and diverse industrial sectors, in alignment with statutory requirements and global best practices.
Graduates will be able to conduct fire and accident investigations, fire safety audits, and root-cause analysis, and plan and manage emergency preparedness, disaster response, and business continuity strategies, aimed at minimizing loss of life, property, and environmental damage.
Graduates will be able to utilize emerging technologies such as simulation tools, data analytics, AI-enabled monitoring systems, IoT-based safety solutions, and fire dynamics modeling software to enhance hazard Identification, prediction, risk mitigation, and decision-making in fire and safety engineering applications.
Graduates will be able to integrate sustainable engineering practices, ethical principles, and human-centric safety approaches while addressing societal, environmental, and industrial safety challenges, contributing to community resilience, environmental protection, and sustainable development goals at local, national, and global levels.

Program Structure
The B.Tech. F&EHS Engineering Program is of Four-year duration. Each year is called an academic year and is divided into two semesters. Thus, there will be a total of eight semesters. Each semester consists of fifteen weeks of teaching. The teaching learning process involves theory classes of an hour duration and practical classes of two hours duration. The curriculum will be delivered through various methods including chalk and talk, laboratories, power point presentations, audio, video tools, E-learning / E-content, virtual laboratories, simulations, Industry visits, seminars, workshops, projects, models, class discussions and industrial internship.
Evaluation
The assessment broadly comprises Internal Assessment (Continuous Evaluation Component) and External Assessment i.e., End Semester Examination. Each course carries 100 marks for theory with 60 marks for Internal Assessment and 40 for End Semester Examination and practical course carries additional 50 marks. The internal assessment will be through MCQ, Classroom Test, Open Book Assignment, Oral Presentation and Short Projects.
Courses
The curriculum structure is based on basic requirement courses in Fire & EHS Engineering. The program consists of Fundamental Engineering courses / Core Courses / Practical Drills/NPTEL Elective / Open Elective courses / Ability and Skill Enhancement Courses/ Value Added Courses and internship/project.
For theory classes, one credit indicates one-hour lecture per week while for practical, one credit indicates two hours practical laboratory session per week.
A student has to earn a minimum of 181 Credits to get a degree in B. Tech in Fire & EHS Engineering.

About FirePlex(AgniSankul)- Centre of Excellence in Fire & EHS
GSFC University has developed the Centre of Excellence in Fire & EHS namely FirePlex (AgniSankul) in the total plot air of 2875sq mtr .The Centre of Excellence will have the facilities like Fire Drill ground, Fire Drill tower, Fire Pump House, Sprinkler room, Foam Spray room, Smoke chamber, Hydrant system, Medium & High Velocity Water Spray System (MVWS, HVWS), Fire alarm system and Gas Flooding system etc. at a single location. The purpose of these facilities is to provide in-depth practical training and live demonstrations to students & Industrial professionals as in the area of fire prevention, fire protection & firefighting.

Eligibility for Admission
For State Quota Seats: The state quota seats of the B. Tech Program will be filled by Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC), Govt. of Gujarat.
For All India Seats (Management Quota): The All-India quota Seats of the B. Tech Program will be filled by GSFC University with the following eligibility criteria:
- Seats (All out of the state applications) will be filled from the all-India candidates (Including Gujarat) on the basis of NTA score in JEE (Main) – 2024 or percentile rank of GUJCET 2024 examinations. Admission announcement for these seats will be posted at the University’s website.
- If seats are not filled under specified JEE & GUJCET, vacant seats will be transferred to either of the category.
- A candidate should have passed (HSC -Science stream), the Qualifying Examinations with minimum 45% marks (40% in case of SC/ST/SEBC) as aggregate in theory and practical of Physics & Maths (with Chemistry or Computer or Vocational Subject) from single board.
Registration Fees
The registration fee for submitting online application form is Rs. 350/- (Rupees Three Hundred Fifty rupees Only), which is to be paid online.
