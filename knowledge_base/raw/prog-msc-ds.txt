About The Program
Powering the Data-Driven Future
In today’s digital era,
data is the new power,
driving decisions, innovation, and global transformation. The
M.Sc. Data Science program at GSFC University
is designed to equip students with a powerful blend of
Mathematics, Statistics and Computer Science,
enabling them to analyze complex data and solve real-world problems.
This
two-year, four-semester program
focuses on applying advanced analytical and computational techniques to
big data,
supported by
hands-on learning and mandatory internships after every semester.
Students gain both theoretical depth and practical expertise, preparing them for careers in
R&D, industry, and academia,
with strong placement support.
Vision - Mission
Department Vision:
To emerge as a nationally recognized hub for Data Science education and applied research, developing ethical, industry-ready professionals who leverage computational intelligence to drive innovation, sustainability, and socio-economic progress.
Department Mission:
Build Strong Foundations:
Impart rigorous training in Statistics, Mathematics, Computing, and Data-driven reasoning to develop technically sound and analytically mature graduates.
Enable Future Technologies:
Equip students with hands-on skills in AI, Machine Learning, and Big Data through experiential pedagogy, real-world datasets, and interdisciplinary applications.
Drive Ethical & Applied Research:
Promote industry-connected, application-oriented research that prioritizes responsible, transparent, and ethical use of data
Develop Industry-Ready Professionals:
Enhance employability through internships, live industry projects, soft-skill training, and continuous curriculum upgrades aligned with evolving technological needs.
Program Outcomes
Advanced Data Science Knowledge:
Apply advanced concepts of data science, statistics, machine learning, and computational intelligence to solve complex real-world problems.
Data Engineering & Computational Skills:
Design and implement scalable data engineering solutions using modern programming languages, cloud platforms, and distributed computing frameworks.
Analytical Thinking & Problem Solving:
Analyze large and complex datasets using critical thinking, statistical inference, and optimization techniques to support data-driven decision-making.
Machine Learning & AI Model Development:
Develop, evaluate, and deploy machine learning, deep learning, and AI models for predictive analytics and intelligent automation.
Data Visualization & Communication:
Communicate analytical findings effectively through professional reports, research publications, dashboards, and data storytelling methods.
Ethics, Privacy & Professional Responsibility:
Evaluate ethical, legal, privacy, and security issues related to data collection, processing, and AI deployment in societal and industrial contexts.
Interdisciplinary Applications & Industry Alignment:
Integrate data science solutions across domains such as healthcare, finance, manufacturing, smart cities, biotechnology, and social sciences.
Research Aptitude & Lifelong Learning:
Demonstrate research competence, innovation, and continuous learning for pursuing Ph.D., entrepreneurship, and emerging technologies in data science.
Specific Outcomes
Advanced Statistical & Mathematical Modelling:
Apply advanced statistical techniques, machine learning theory, optimization, and probabilistic modelling for complex data analysis.
Big Data Analytics & Data Engineering:
Design and manage big data pipelines using tools such as Hadoop, Spark, NoSQL databases, and cloud-based architectures.
AI, Deep Learning & Intelligent Systems:
Develop deep learning models and AI-driven applications using neural networks, NLP, computer vision, and reinforcement learning approaches.
Business Intelligence & Decision Support Systems:
Build decision-support solutions using data visualization, dashboards, and predictive analytics for strategic organizational planning.
Research-Based Project & Industry Solutions:
Execute industry-oriented projects and research dissertations involving real-world datasets, model deployment, and scholarly reporting.

Evaluation
The overall assessment comprises an Internal Assessment (60% weightage) and an External End-Semester Examination (ESE, 40% weightage). The Internal Assessment is split into a Mid-Semester Examination (MSE) and Continuous Evaluation Components (CEC). Each theory course carries 100 marks, while practical courses are evaluated out of 50 marks. CEC evaluations include MCQ tests, open-book exams, assignments, article reviews, oral presentations, and short projects.
Courses
The curriculum of the program is based on the UGC prescribed curriculum structure and as per NEP 2020 policy. The courses and allied credits are distributed in major, minor, inter-multidisciplinary, ability/skill enhancement, research, etc. categories as per the prescribed standards.

Candidates with ≥55% in B.Sc./B.E./B.Tech/BCA are eligible for
direct admission through the interview process.
The candidate must register through the University website for getting notified for the interview process.
Final-year students from eligible disciplines may apply

merit-based,

Eligible backgrounds include
Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, Economics, Econometrics, Operational Research, Engineering (CS/IT/Electronics/Electrical), BCA, B.Sc. (IT), and B.Sc. (Data Science).
Final-year students may also apply, subject to submission of results.
