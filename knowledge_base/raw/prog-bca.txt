About The Program
Embark on a transformative journey through the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) Program at GSFC University, a dynamic degree meticulously crafted to empower you with a strong foundational knowledge of computer applications, cutting-edge theories in information technology, and proficient programming skills. Immerse yourself in a comprehensive curriculum that explores diverse domains, including digital computer fundamentals, computational thinking, Linux/Unix Programming, C programming, data structures, Java Programming, database management systems, and security. Program Structure
The BCA Program has a duration of four years, with the option for students to exit after three years. Each academic year is divided into two semesters, resulting in a total of eight semesters. The duration of each semester is fifteen weeks, dedicated to teaching and learning.
The teaching-learning process incorporates both theory classes, which are one hour in duration, and practical classes, which are four hours long. The curriculum delivery encompasses various methods, including traditional chalk and talk lectures, hands-on laboratory sessions, dynamic presentations using audio and video tools, engaging e-learning and e-content resources, virtual laboratories, simulations, field trips, industry visits, seminars, workshops, projects, interactive models, class discussions, and invaluable industrial internships.
Evaluation
Evaluation methods encompass both internal and external assessments. The assessment structure comprises Internal Assessment, which is a continuous evaluation component, and External Assessment, specifically the End Semester Examination. Each course is allocated a total of 100 marks for theory, with 50 marks allocated for Internal Assessment and 50 marks for the End Semester Examination. Additionally, practical courses carry an extra 50 marks. Internal assessment is conducted through multiple methods such as Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs), classroom tests, assignments, oral presentations, and short projects.

Eligibility for Admission
Candidate must have passed 12th or equivalent Examination from the recognized Board excluding NIOS securing a minimum 65% and above mark in aggregate of all the subjects. Applicants with 12th or equivalent from International Schools, affiliated to foreign education boards like IGCSE (International General Certificate for Secondary Education) and IB (International Baccalaureate) can apply, they have to submit equivalence certificate of Association of Indian Universities, New Delhi.
Registration Fees
The registration fee for submitting online application form is Rs. 500/- (Rupees Five Hundred Only), which is to be paid online.
Selection Process:
60% and above aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - Direct admission on first cum first basis. 60% to 65% aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - on the spot Admission after interaction with the Faculty, School of Science, GSFC University.
