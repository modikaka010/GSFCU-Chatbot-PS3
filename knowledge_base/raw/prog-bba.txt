About The Program
This Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) program is designed in the spirit of NEP 2020 to provide a broad, multidisciplinary, skill-oriented business education. It integrates conceptual business knowledge, digital skills, internships, and ability-enhancement courses so that graduates can perform in a rapidly changing business environment.
The BBA program provides a comprehensive grounding in management principles, quantitative skills, business communication, and functional areas such as accounting, marketing, HR, and analytics. It prepares students for entry-level business roles or for higher studies in management and allied fields.
Program Outcomes
Graduates will be industry-ready and able to adapt to emerging business trends.
Graduates will be proficient in business communication and in the use of contemporary digital tools.
Graduates will possess managerial and leadership competencies to support their professional and personal growth.
Graduates will demonstrate exposure to real business situations and a commitment to lifelong learning.
Program Objectives
To enable students to identify, analyze, and solve business problems using appropriate tools..
To develop professionalism, ethics, business communication, teamwork, and a multidisciplinary outlook.
To prepare students for careers in industry, entrepreneurship, and higher education with a focus on creativity and commitment
To create awareness of the social, environmental, and human dimensions of business decisions

Program Structure
The BBA Program is of it’s 3 years / 6 semesters (or 4 years / 8 semesters under NEP with Hons. / Research). Each year is called an academic year and is divided into two semesters. Thus, there will be a total of six/eight semesters. Each semester consists of fifteen weeks of teaching. The teaching–learning process includes lectures, tutorials, computer-based sessions, case discussions, presentations, simulations, field/industry visits, and project work.
Evaluation
The assessment broadly comprises Internal Assessment (Continuous Evaluation Component) and External Assessment i.e., End Semester Examination. Each course carries 100 marks for theory with 60 marks for Internal Assessment and 40 marks for End Semester Examination. The internal assessment (CEC - Continuous Evaluation Components) will be through MCQs, classroom tests, assignments, oral presentations, and projects.
Courses
The curriculum structure is based on AICTE syllabus. The program consists of Core Courses (CC) / Discipline Specific Electives (DSE) / Generic Electives Courses of six credits each and Skill Enhancement Courses of two credits each.
For theory classes, one credit indicates one hour of lecture per week while for practical, one credit indicates two hours of practical laboratory sessions per week.
The curriculum follows the UGC-recommended CBCS pattern and includes: Core Courses (CC), Discipline-Specific Electives (DSE), Generic/Multidisciplinary Electives, Ability Enhancement Compulsory Courses (AECC), and Skill Enhancement Courses (SEC).
An industry internship / field attachment is a mandatory component of the program and will be conducted in specified semesters as per the detailed syllabus.

Eligibility for Admission
Candidate must have passed 12th or equivalent (All Streams) Examination from the recognized Board excluding NIOS securing a minimum 45% mark in aggregate of all the subjects. (40% for SC/ST/OBC-Non-Creamy Layer / PD / EWS). Applicants with 12th or equivalent from International Schools, affiliated to foreign education boards like IGCSE (International General Certificate for Secondary Education) and IB (International Baccalaureate) can apply, they have to submit equivalence certificate of Association of Indian Universities, New Delhi.
Registration Fees
The registration fee for submitting online application form is Rs. 500/- (Rupees Five Hundred Only), which is to be paid online.
Selection Process:
50% and above aggregate marks in H.S.C (12th Std) or equivalent - Admission will be granted on a merit / first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability of seats.
