OUR USPs
GSFC University
, a CSR initiative of
Gujarat State Fertilizers and Chemicals Ltd.
(GSFC Ltd.), is a
boutique university
committed to developing
future-ready
and
highly employable graduates
through
industry integration, innovation
and a
student-centric approach
. With a
futuristic outlook
, it fosters
creativity, academic excellence
and
pedagogical innovation
, supported by an
agile and flexible learning ecosystem
. The University emphasizes
all-round development
and
industry readiness
, preparing students to excel in a
dynamic global environment
Our Unique Selling Propositions
(USPs)
1. FirePlex | Agnisankul
A dedicated hub for
fire safety
and
industrial risk management excellence
It combines
advanced training
simulation-based learning
, and
industry practices
to build expertise in
fire safety
and
emergency response
2. Student Internship Program (SIP)
mandatory industrial internship every semester
, accounting for
15–20% of the program duration
, with
no semester breaks
The internship is
seamlessly integrated
and
aligned with the academic curriculum
3. Faculty Internship Program (FIP)
Faculty complete
80 hours of annual industry immersion
to stay updated with
current practices
and deliver
industry-relevant, practical, and impactful learning
This ensures a
strong industry-academia connect
and enhances
students’ real-world readiness
4. State-of-the-Art Laboratories
The campus houses
45+ advanced laboratories
, including the specialized
Anviksha building
dedicated to
cutting-edge research and development
These facilities enable
hands-on learning
innovation
, and
practical skill enhancement
aligned with
industry standards
5. Student Managed Clubs (SMCs)
Over
25 student-led clubs
that foster
leadership, creativity, and innovation
among students.
These platforms encourage
teamwork
initiative
, and
all-round development
beyond the classroom.
6. GUIITAR Council
The
Incubation, Innovation, Technology, and Applied Research ecosystem
that fosters
creativity
, supports
startups
, and drives
research-led innovation
, transforming ideas into
impactful solutions
It provides
mentorship
resources
, and
industry connections
to nurture
entrepreneurial thinking
and
real-world problem solving
7. President’s Initiative for Nurturing Unlimited Potential of Students (PINUPS)
Focused on
personalized growth
and
all-round development
It offers
tailored mentoring
skill enhancement
, and
continuous guidance
to help students achieve their
full potential
The initiative empowers students to excel
academically, professionally, and personally
8. Student Participative Management
Active student involvement in
governance
through roles in
BOS, Bhavan Council, and Mess Council
This approach fosters
leadership
accountability
, and a sense of
ownership in decision-making
9. Digital Campus System (DCS)
smart, simple, and seamless digital platform
for
academic and administrative processes
It ensures
efficient access to information
transparency
, and a
streamlined campus experience
10. Multidisciplinary Education
Future-ready programs that integrate
science, technology, management, and humanities
Aligned with
NEP 2020
, it promotes
flexibility
choice-based learning
, and
all-round development
This approach fosters
critical thinking
innovation
, and
adaptability
to meet
global and industry demands
11. Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM) & Townhall Meetings (TM)
Platforms for
transparent communication
and
meaningful academic engagement
among
parents, faculty, and students
They enable
open dialogue with senior management
, fostering
trust
collaboration
, and

5-week transformative induction program

It builds strong foundations in
values
communication
life skills
, and
academic readiness

Students act as

academic engagement
peer learning
, and
collaborative knowledge building

Approximately
30% of courses

industry experts

practical insights
real-world exposure
